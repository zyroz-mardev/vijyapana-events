About Vijyapana Events

Vijyapana has spent years building brands across Kanpur: the hoardings, the films, the packaging, the launches. Vijyapana Events brings that same team to the moments people remember.

Vijyapana comes from विज्ञापन, Hindi for advertisement. Our agency already runs a design studio, a print unit, a film crew and a gifting team.

  1. I. One point of contact

    A single coordinator owns your event, from the first call to the last guest leaving.

  2. II. Designed before it’s built

    Moodboards, 3D renders and floor plans. You approve them before any work starts.

  3. III. Made in-house

    Printing, films, stage visuals and gifts come from our own teams, so everything matches.

  4. IV. One clear costing

    A single, line-by-line estimate. No surprise bills from ten different vendors.

  5. V. Run to the minute

    A written run sheet and a floor team that keeps every moment on time.
