Four days, as we usually run them

Every family changes the order. This is where most of ours start.

  1. 12:00
    Welcome lunchGuests come in from the airport to lunch on the terrace. Rooms are keyed before they land.
  2. 16:00
    Mehendi in the zenana courtyardSix artists, so nobody waits longer than twenty minutes.
  3. 20:00
    Folk dinnerManganiyar singers and a dal-baati supper under the trees.
  1. 10:00
    Haldi by the poolMarigold, turmeric, and a change of clothes laid out in every room.
  2. 19:30
    SangeetA stage, a band and the family dances, rehearsed with a choreographer the week before.
  3. 23:00
    After-party on the lawnThe sound comes down at one, which keeps the palace on our side.
  1. 17:30
    BaraatThe groom’s side leaves the hotel with a band, a horse and a timed route.
  2. 19:00
    Varmala at the gateLit for photographs, with both families in place before the groom arrives.
  3. 21:15
    PherasUnder the mandap at the muhurat your pandit gives us, to the minute.
  1. 12:00
    Family brunchA slow one, after a late night.
  2. 19:30
    Reception on the lake terraceEight hundred diyas lit at dusk, and a receiving line that moves.
  3. 10:00
    Vidaai, the next morningCars, luggage and the airport run, handled while the families say goodbye.

We move ceremonies around your muhurat, not the other way round.
